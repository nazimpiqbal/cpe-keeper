How long to keep CPE records, by state

Updated November 19, 2026

Boards audit a sample of renewals each cycle, and an audit asks for certificates, not just a list of courses. Most states expect you to keep proof for three to six years.

StateRecord keeping
AlabamaKeep CPE records for 5 years after completion.
AlaskaKeep CPE documentation for at least 4 years (renewal form).
ArizonaKeep CPE records for three years.
ArkansasKeep CPE records for five years after the end of the year completed.
CaliforniaKeep CPE records for 4 years.
ColoradoKeep CPE documentation for at least 5 years from the end of the year the CPE was completed.
ConnecticutKeep documentation for at least 3 years from each program's completion.
DelawareKeep CPE evidence for 5 years after completing each activity (§7.2.9).
FloridaKeep proof for at least two years after each two-year period.
GeorgiaKeep documentation for 5 years after each program.
HawaiiKeep certificates in case you're chosen for audit.
IdahoNot stated in the rules we track; keep records at least through your next renewal and any audit.
IllinoisKeep CPE records for at least 6 years.
IndianaKeep certificates of completion for 36 months after the end of the renewal period.
IowaKeep CPE records for 3 years after submitting the report that claims them.
KansasKeep CPE records for six years from the end of the year completed.
KentuckyKeep CPE records for 5 years.
LouisianaKeep CPE documentation (and course materials showing content and instructors) for five full calendar years after each program.
MaineKeep CPE documentation for the three license years before the current one (Ch. 5 §8(D)).
MarylandKeep CPE documentation for four years.
MassachusettsKeep certificates and course details; you sign a statement under penalty of perjury at renewal and the Board can ask for proof.
MichiganKeep CE records for 4 years after applying for renewal (R 338.5210).
MinnesotaKeep CPE documentation for 5 years.
MississippiKeep CPE records for at least 5 years after the end of the compliance year and triennium.
MissouriKeep CPE documentation for at least 5 calendar years after the year completed (20 CSR 2010-4.031).
MontanaKeep CPE records for at least 5 years. Audits go through the NASBA CPE Audit Service.
NebraskaKeep CPE records for six years.
NevadaKeep CPE records for 3 years after filing the renewal.
New HampshireKeep CPE records for at least 4 years from the renewal date.
New JerseyNot stated in the rules we track; keep records at least through your next renewal and any audit.
New MexicoKeep CPE records for five years after completion or reporting.
New YorkKeep CPE records for 5 years.
North CarolinaKeep CPE records for the current year and the four calendar years before it.
North DakotaKeep CPE records for 5 calendar years after the year you took the course. CPE is reported through the Board's Certemy portal.
OhioNot stated in the rules we track; keep records at least through your next renewal and any audit.
OklahomaKeep CPE records for at least 5 years from the end of the year completed.
OregonKeep CPE records for 5 years after completing each course.
PennsylvaniaKeep certificates for 5 years after each program (§ 11.68(b)).
Rhode IslandKeep CPE documentation for the current and the previous renewal period (§1.8.1(E)).
South CarolinaKeep CPE records for at least 5 years from the reporting due date.
South DakotaKeep CPE documentation in case of the Board's yearly audit (ARSD 20:75:04:14).
TennesseeKeep CPE documentation for five years after completion.
TexasKeep CPE records for 5 years.
UtahKeep CPE certificates; reporting goes through NASBA's CPE Audit Service.
VermontKeep CPE documentation for 5 years after each program (Rule 9.3(b)).
VirginiaKeep CPE documentation for the four calendar years before the current year.
WashingtonNot stated in the rules we track; keep records at least through your next renewal and any audit.
West VirginiaThe Board keeps CPE reports for 4 years; keep your certificates at least that long.
WisconsinKeep CPE records in case of a Board audit.
WyomingKeep CPE evidence for 4 years after completion.

What to keep

For each course, keep the certificate of completion. NASBA requires Registry sponsors' certificates to show your name, the program title, field(s) of study, the date and location, the delivery method, the credit earned, the sponsor's name and address, the sponsor's Registry ID, and a signature from the person responsible for the sponsor's CPE programs. If you hold more than one license, keep records for the longest period any of your states requires.
