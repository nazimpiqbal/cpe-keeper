What CPE Keeper does, and what it doesn't

CPE Keeper helps accountants record continuing professional education (CPE) and compare it with the requirements published by state boards of accountancy. It is a record-keeping and tracking tool.

It is not legal, regulatory or professional advice, and it does not replace your board's rules. Requirements change, boards interpret their own rules, and your situation may involve details the app doesn't capture. You are responsible for knowing and meeting your board's requirements, for checking that each course qualifies, and for reporting your CPE accurately. Each state's summary in the app links to the board sources it is based on; if anything in the app differs from what your board says, your board is right. We work to keep the rules current but don't guarantee that they are complete, accurate or up to date.

Course details read from certificates and transcripts are produced automatically and can be wrong. Review each course before you save it.
