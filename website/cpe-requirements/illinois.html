Illinois CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 2, 2026

Board
Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR)
CPE required
120 hours every 3 years
Renewal
Illinois licensed CPAs renew every three years on September 30.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for at least 6 years.

How it works

Licensed CPAs renew every three years; the period runs October 1 – September 30. No carryover.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE120every 3 years 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Personal developmentUp to 24every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 24 personal development hours count toward the 120.
Self-studyUp to 80every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 80 hours of self-study (interactive and non-interactive combined) count per renewal period, and no more than 60 of them non-interactive (68 Ill. Adm. Code 1420.70).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics4every 3 years 
Sexual harassment prevention1every 3 years1 hour each renewal (20 ILCS 2105/2105-15.5). It counts toward the 120.

New licensees

Licensed CPAs are exempt from CPE for the first renewal after the license is issued.

Other limits

  • Non-interactive self-study: max 60 of the 80 self-study hours (certificates rarely say which).
  • Non-verifiable CPE: max 60 hours.
  • Instruction: max 60 hours.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation (IDFPR) whether that applies to you.
