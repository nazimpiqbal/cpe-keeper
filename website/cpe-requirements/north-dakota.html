North Dakota CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
North Dakota State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
120 hours over 3 calendar years
Renewal
North Dakota licenses renew every year by June 30, with your CPE report.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 5 calendar years after the year you took the course. CPE is reported through the Board's Certemy portal.

How it works

North Dakota reporting years run July 1 – June 30, with CPE reported at renewal by June 30. Each year needs 20 hours; the rolling three years need 120, including 6 of ethics.

Requirements

Minimum each year20each calendar yearAt least 20 hours every reporting year (July 1 – June 30).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics6over 3 calendar years6 hours of ethics in the rolling three reporting years. No North Dakota-specific course is required.

New licensees

No CPE is due for the reporting year you were certified in. After that, 20 hours a year; the 120 and the 6 ethics hours apply once three full reporting years have passed.

Other limits

  • Credit is earned in fifths of an hour (10 minutes = 0.2).
  • Teaching: up to twice the program credit, not for repeat presentations.
  • Only a certificate of attendance or completion counts as proof.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the North Dakota State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
