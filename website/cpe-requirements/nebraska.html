Nebraska CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Nebraska Board of Public Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
December 31 before your June 30 renewal year (e.g. 12/31/2026 for a June 2027 renewal).
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for six years.

How it works

Nebraska permits renew every two years by June 30. CPE comes from the two calendar years before the renewal year: 80 hours including 4 ethics, due December 31 and reported by January 31.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours in the two calendar years before renewal (288 NAC 8).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Self-studyUp to 40every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 40 self-study hours count each period.
Personal developmentUp to 16every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 16 personal development hours count each period.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4every 2 years4 hours of ethics each period (general or professional ethics, independence, Circular 230 and similar).

New licensees

Permit issued during this period: 80 hours, or 40 if it was issued after July 1 of the year before it expires

Other limits

  • Instruction: max 40 hours; firm meetings: max 16.
  • Audit, review or compilation work: 40% A&A is recommended, not required.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Nebraska Board of Public Accountancy whether that applies to you.
