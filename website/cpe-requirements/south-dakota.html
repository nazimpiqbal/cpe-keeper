South Dakota CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
South Dakota Board of Accountancy (Department of Labor & Regulation)
CPE required
120 hours over 3 calendar years
Renewal
South Dakota certificates renew every year by August 1 (enter 07/31). CPE years run July 1 – June 30.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation in case of the Board's yearly audit (ARSD 20:75:04:14).

How it works

South Dakota counts CPE in July 1 – June 30 years: 120 hours over the rolling three years and at least 20 each year. Certificates renew every year by August 1. There's no ethics requirement.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Behavioral or motivationalUp to 24over 3 calendar yearsApplies to: I work in public accounting. Maximum, not a target. In public accounting, no more than 24 behavioral or motivational hours count per three years (ARSD 20:75:04:09).

New licensees

No CPE is due for the CPE year you were certified in. Then at least 20 hours a year; the 120 over three years applies from the third full year (ARSD 20:75:04:07).

Other limits

  • University or college teaching: max 60 hours per three years.
  • Published work needs Board approval first.
  • Self-study and nano-learning must come from NASBA Registry sponsors.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the South Dakota Board of Accountancy (Department of Labor & Regulation) whether that applies to you.
