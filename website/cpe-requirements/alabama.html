Alabama CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 9, 2026

Board
Alabama State Board of Public Accountancy
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year
Renewal
Alabama annual permits expire every September 30. The renewal application and fee are due by December 31. CPE years run October–September.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 5 years after completion.

How it works

Alabama CPE years run October 1 – September 30: 40 hours, including 2 ethics and 8 accounting & auditing (30-X-5, amended effective Oct 15, 2026). Annual permits expire September 30; the renewal application and fee are due by December 31.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Annual CPE40each calendar year40 hours each fiscal year ending September 30 (30-X-5-.02).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Accounting & auditing8each calendar yearAt least 8 of the 40 in accounting and auditing — required of every active CPA.
Personal developmentUp to 12each calendar yearMaximum, not a target. No more than 12 personal development hours count.
Nano-learningUp to 10each calendar yearMaximum, not a target. Nano-learning is limited to 25% (10 hours).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics2each calendar yearAt least 2 hours in a qualifying ethics course every year.

New licensees

If you went active within 5 years of passing the CPA exam, your first 40 hours are due by the end of the first full CPE year: the September 30 one year after the September 30 following your election (30-X-5-.02(2)). Courses count from October 1.

Other limits

  • Teaching: twice the lecture hours, no credit for repeats.
  • Software courses count as A&A only if about accounting or audit applications.
  • Publications: up to 50% of the requirement (more with Board approval).
  • Credit is in 50-minute hours; after the first hour, in 1/5 or 1/2 hour steps. Self-study minimum 25 minutes; nano-learning minimum 10 minutes at 1/5 hour.
  • Going active again, or more than 5 years after the exam: 40 hours for each non-active year (max 120) in the 3 years before, with 20% A&A and 5% ethics.
  • Non-residents may meet their principal-office state's CPE rules instead, if that state has CPE.
  • A failed CPE audit means the Board also audits your next two fiscal years.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Alabama State Board of Public Accountancy whether that applies to you.
