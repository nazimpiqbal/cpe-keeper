Oregon CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Oregon Board of Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Oregon licenses expire June 30 every two years.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 5 years after completing each course.

How it works

Oregon licenses renew every two years on June 30. CPE counts from the two years before: 80 hours, at least 20 each year (July–June), including 4 hours of Oregon ethics.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each renewal period (OAR 801-040). Up to 20 extra hours from your previous period carry forward — not toward the yearly minimum.
Total hours each year20each year 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Non-technicalUp to 16every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 16 non-technical hours count each period.
Nano-learningUp to 8every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. Nano-learning is limited to 10% of the required hours (8).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Oregon ethics4every 2 years4 hours of Oregon-specific ethics each period (Oregon statutes and rules, the Board's Code of Professional Conduct). If your principal office is in another state with an ethics requirement, those hours can count instead.

New licensees

First period prorated at 3⅓ hours a month, counting the month your license was issued (OAR 801-040-0100)

Other limits

  • Teaching and published articles combined: max half the hours.
  • Municipal auditors: 24 governmental hours per period (16 in governmental auditing).
  • Carried-forward hours can't be non-technical.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Oregon Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
