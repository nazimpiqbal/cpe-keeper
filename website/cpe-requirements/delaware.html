Delaware CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Delaware Board of Accountancy (Division of Professional Regulation)
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Delaware permits expire June 30 of odd-numbered years (e.g. 06/30/2027).
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE evidence for 5 years after completing each activity (§7.2.9).

How it works

Delaware permits expire June 30 of odd-numbered years. Each period needs 80 hours, at least 20 each year, with 8 accounting or auditing, 8 tax, 36 in accounting, auditing or tax overall, and a 4-hour Delaware ethics course.

Accounting, auditing or tax36every 2 yearsAt least 36 of the 80 in accounting, auditing or tax: the 8 + 8 below plus 20 more in any of them.

Self-studyUp to 24every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. Self-study counts for no more than 30% of the requirement (24 of 80) (§7.2.8).
Nano-learningUp to 4every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. Nano-learning counts for no more than 5% of the requirement (4 of 80).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Delaware ethics4every 2 yearsA 4-hour Board-approved Delaware ethics course each period (§7.2.6).

Other limits

  • Instructor/developer credit: max 50%; author credit: max 25%.
  • Specialized group learning and technical committees combined: max 25%.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Delaware Board of Accountancy (Division of Professional Regulation) whether that applies to you.
