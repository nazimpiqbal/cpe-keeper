Rhode Island CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Rhode Island Board of Accountancy (Department of Business Regulation)
CPE required
120 hours every 3 years
Renewal
Rhode Island licenses expire June 30 every three years; your license shows which year.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for the current and the previous renewal period (§1.8.1(E)).

How it works

Rhode Island licenses run three years ending June 30: 120 hours, at least 6 of them ethics. No yearly minimum.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE120every 3 years120 hours each three-year period (400-RICR-00-00-1.8.1).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Self-studyUp to 80every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 80 self-study hours count per period (§1.8.1(D)).
Personal development and marketingUp to 24every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 24 hours of personal development or marketing per period (§1.8.2(C)).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics6every 3 yearsAt least 6 hours (4 in your first cycle) covering regulatory and behavioral ethics (§1.8.2(E)). No Rhode Island-specific course is required.

Other limits

  • Teaching: max 60 hours; authorship: max 60 hours.
  • If your main office is in another state, meeting that state's rules satisfies Rhode Island's.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Rhode Island Board of Accountancy (Department of Business Regulation) whether that applies to you.
