Texas CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on September 30, 2026

Board
Texas State Board of Public Accountancy (TSBPA)
CPE required
120 hours every 3 years
Renewal
Texas licenses renew every year on the last day of your birth month.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 5 years.

How it works

Licenses renew every year on the last day of your birth month. Each renewal looks back 36 months (120 hours) and 12 months (20 hours). No carryover.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical60every 3 yearsAt least half of the required credits must be technical, because no more than half can be non-technical (§ 523.118(a)). Technical: accounting, attest, tax, consulting, economics, finance, IT, regulatory ethics.
Non-technicalUp to 60every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than half of the required credits can be non-technical (§ 523.118(a)): communications, computer software and applications, behavioral ethics, business management and organization.
Nano-learningUp to 60every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than half of the required credits can come from nano-learning (§ 523.118(b)).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Board-approved Texas ethics course4in the last 24 monthsA 4-hour TSBPA-approved ethics course every two years. Out-of-state licensees with no Texas clients may meet their home state's ethics rule instead.

New licensees

Texas phases CPE in over your first years of licensure (Rule 523.112).

  • No CPE is required for your first partial license period or your first full 12-month license period.
  • Second full license year: 20 hours in the 12 months before renewal.
  • Third full license year: 60 hours in the 24 months before renewal, at least 20 in the last 12.
  • Fourth full license year: 100 hours in the 36 months before renewal, at least 20 in the last 12.

Other limits

  • Nano-learning: max 50% of required hours in three years.
  • Certification programs (CFP, CIA, CFE…): max 50% in three years.
  • Instruction credit: max 20 hours a year.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Texas State Board of Public Accountancy (TSBPA) whether that applies to you.
