Alaska CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Alaska Board of Public Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Alaska licenses expire December 31 of odd-numbered years (e.g. 12/31/2027).
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for at least 4 years (renewal form).

How it works

Alaska licenses expire December 31 of odd-numbered years. CPE: 80 hours over the two calendar years, at least 20 in each year, 4 of them ethics (12 AAC 04.300).

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each two-year period (12 AAC 04.300(c)).
Total hours each year20each year 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Micro-learningUp to 10every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. Activities shorter than an hour (micro-learning) count for no more than 10 hours a period (12 AAC 04.300(e)).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4every 2 years4 hours of ethics each period (12 AAC 04.300(d)). Any approved ethics course counts; no Alaska-specific course is required.

New licensees

Licenses first issued during this period need no CPE for their first renewal (Board renewal form).

Other limits

  • From 2026: no more than 12 hours count for any one day.
  • Instructor credit: max 30 hours a period (preparation up to twice the teaching time).
  • Published articles and books no longer earn credit.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Alaska Board of Public Accountancy whether that applies to you.
