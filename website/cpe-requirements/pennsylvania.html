Pennsylvania CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 1, 2026

Board
Pennsylvania State Board of Accountancy (Department of State)
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Pennsylvania licenses expire December 31 of odd-numbered years (e.g. 12/31/2027).
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep certificates for 5 years after each program (§ 11.68(b)).

How it works

Licenses expire December 31 of odd-numbered years; CPE runs January 1 of the even year to December 31 of the odd year.

New licensees

Passed the CPA exam during this reporting period? You're exempt from CPE for your first renewal (Department of State renewal guidance; § 11.62(a) applies the same exemption to the initial license).

Other limits

  • Instruction: max 40 hours (3 CPE per 50 minutes presented).
  • Authorship: 20 per publication, 40 total (shares the 40-hour self-study cap).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Pennsylvania State Board of Accountancy (Department of State) whether that applies to you.
