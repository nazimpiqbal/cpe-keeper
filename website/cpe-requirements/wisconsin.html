Wisconsin CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 4, 2026

Board
Wisconsin Accounting Examining Board
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Wisconsin licenses expire December 14 of odd-numbered years (e.g. 12/14/2027).
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE records in case of a Board audit.

How it works

Wisconsin licenses expire December 14 of odd-numbered years. CPE runs December 15 – December 14: 80 hours over the two years, at least 20 in each year, 3 of ethics.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics3every 2 years3 hours of ethics from formal learning activities each period. Regulatory and behavioral ethics both count.

Other limits

  • At least 40 of the 80 from formal learning activities (programs from qualifying sponsors — most courses with certificates qualify).
  • Carryback: hours earned in the first 12 months of a period can instead count for the previous period.
  • Up to 3 ethics hours over the previous period's requirement can carry forward toward this period's ethics (Board guidance, 2020).
  • Carried hours can also count toward a year's 20-hour minimum (Board guidance).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Wisconsin Accounting Examining Board whether that applies to you.
