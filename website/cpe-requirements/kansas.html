Kansas CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Kansas Board of Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
June 30 before your permit renews on July 1 (odd or even years by your certificate number).
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for six years from the end of the year completed.

How it works

Kansas permits renew every two years on July 1; CPE runs July 1 – June 30 over the two years before: 80 hours, 2 of them ethics.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each permit period (K.A.R. 74-4-7). Up to 20 extra hours carry forward (not toward ethics).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Personal developmentUp to 24every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. Personal development is limited to 30% (24 hours).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics2every 2 years2 hours of professional ethics relating to CPA practice each period.

New licensees

First period prorated: 10 hours for each full quarter after your permit was issued

Other limits

  • Instruction and preparation: max 50%; college credit: max 50%.
  • Municipal public accountants: 16 hours a year, 8 in municipal A&A.
  • Missing CPE adds 8 extra hours.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Kansas Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
