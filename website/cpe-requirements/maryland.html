Maryland CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 4, 2026

Board
Maryland State Board of Public Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Maryland licenses renew every two years on your license's own date.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for four years.

How it works

Maryland licenses run two years. Each renewal needs 80 hours from the license period, including 4 of ethics; no yearly minimum.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each license period. Up to 80 hours over the 80 from your previous period carry forward (they count toward the total, not toward ethics).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics4every 2 years4 hours of professional ethics every license period. Extra ethics hours count as general CPE but can't carry forward as ethics.

New licensees

First renewal: no CPE is due. Report what you took anyway — only reported hours can carry forward to your next period.

Other limits

  • Instruction: max 45 hours per period, first presentation only (COMAR 09.24.02.04G).
  • Published material: max 10 hours per publication, 40 per period (COMAR 09.24.02.04F).
  • Peer review: max 16 hours per period (12 for an engagement review, 16 for a system review) (COMAR 09.24.02.04E).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Maryland State Board of Public Accountancy whether that applies to you.
