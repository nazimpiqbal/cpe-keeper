New Hampshire CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
New Hampshire Board of Accountancy (OPLC)
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Two years from your license's issue or last renewal date. (Some licenses from the 2023 transition run three years and need 120 hours.)
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for at least 4 years from the renewal date.

How it works

New Hampshire licenses run two years from their own issue date (Ac 401.03): 80 hours each period, at least 20 in each year, 4 of them ethics, up to 40 carried forward.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each two-year period (Ac 403.01). Up to 40 hours over the 80 carry to the next period only.
Total hours each year20each yearAll licensees: at least 20 hours each year (Ac 403.01(c)).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Nano-learningUp to 6every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 3 nano-learning hours a year (6 per period).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics4every 2 yearsAll licensees: at least 4 hours of professional ethics each period (Ac 403.01(d)). No New Hampshire-specific course is required.

New licensees

New licensees: at least 20 hours for each year before renewal

Other limits

  • Authoring: max 50%; instructing: max 50% (preparation up to twice the presentation time).
  • Three-year transition licenses: 120 hours, carry up to 60 — not modelled.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the New Hampshire Board of Accountancy (OPLC) whether that applies to you.
