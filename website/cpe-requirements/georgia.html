Georgia CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 1, 2026

Board
Georgia State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Georgia licenses renew December 31 of odd-numbered years (e.g. 12/31/2027). Licensees 70 or older are exempt.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep documentation for 5 years after each program.

How it works

Licenses renew December 31 of odd-numbered years; the reporting period is the two calendar years before.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 yearsUp to 15 extra non-technical credits from the previous reporting period count toward the 80 (not toward technical or yearly minimums).
Total hours each year20each year 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical fields40every 2 yearsAt least half of the credits in technical fields: accounting, auditing, business law, economics, finance, IT, management services, regulatory ethics, specialized knowledge, statistics, taxes.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4every 2 years 
Georgia-specific ethics1every 2 years1 of the 4 ethics credits must cover the laws, rules and policies of the Georgia Board.

New licensees

Not required in the year you were licensed.

Other limits

  • Instruction, authorship and technical-reviewer credit: max 50% each.
  • Age 70+: exempt from CPE.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Georgia State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
