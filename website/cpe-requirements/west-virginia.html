West Virginia CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
West Virginia Board of Accountancy
CPE required
120 hours over 3 calendar years
Renewal
West Virginia licenses renew every year by June 30. CPE is tracked by calendar year.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
The Board keeps CPE reports for 4 years; keep your certificates at least that long.

How it works

West Virginia counts CPE by calendar year: at least 20 hours each year and 120 over the rolling three years, 4 of them ethics. Each year is reported by January 31.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Three-year total120over 3 calendar years120 hours in the three calendar years ending this year (1CSR1 §1-1-7.3).
Minimum each year20each calendar yearAt least 20 hours every calendar year. The Board recommends 40.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4over 3 calendar yearsAt least 4 hours of ethics in the three years (ethical reasoning, West Virginia statutes and rules, professional conduct).

New licensees

No CPE is due for the calendar year you were licensed. The next year needs 40 hours, then 20 a year; the 120 over three years applies from the third full year.

Other limits

  • Instructor credit: 3 hours per hour taught the first time, max 60; publications: max 60.
  • Principles-of-accounting courses don't count.
  • Audits require original certificates.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the West Virginia Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
