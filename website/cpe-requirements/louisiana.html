Louisiana CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 9, 2026

Board
Louisiana State Board of Certified Public Accountants
CPE required
80 hours over 2 calendar years
Renewal
Louisiana certificates renew every year by December 31. CPE is tracked by calendar year.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation (and course materials showing content and instructors) for five full calendar years after each program.

How it works

Louisiana CPE runs by calendar year, completed by December 31 and reported online by January 31: at least 20 hours a year and 80 over the rolling two calendar years, plus a Board-approved ethics course in even-numbered years. New licensees: nothing in the year licensed, 20 the first full year, then the 80. No carryover; nano-learning doesn't count.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Louisiana ethics3each calendar yearIn even-numbered years, a Board-approved Louisiana ethics course (3 hours in 2026). General ethics doesn't count.

New licensees

No CPE is due in the calendar year you were licensed. Your first full year needs at least 20 hours; the two-year 80 starts the year after (Louisiana Board, Oct 2026).

Other limits

  • Published materials: max 10 hours a year; teaching/speaking (with preparation): max 20; credentialing exams: max 20.
  • Self-study must be interactive (most NASBA QAS courses are).
  • Personal development and nano-learning over the limit come off this year's hours; earlier years' excess isn't re-checked.
  • Credit is in 50-minute hours, rounded down to the nearest half hour.
  • Louisiana ethics isn't needed if you mainly practice in another state that has its own ethics requirement and hold an active license there.
  • Non-residents can instead meet their principal-place-of-business state's CPE rules (signed statement at renewal).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Louisiana State Board of Certified Public Accountants whether that applies to you.
