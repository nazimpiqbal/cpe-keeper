Kentucky CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Kentucky State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
60 hours every 2 years (80 if you work at a CPA firm)
Renewal
December 31 of the year before your renewal year (e.g. 12/31/2026 for a 2027 renewal).
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 5 years.

How it works

Kentucky licenses renew every two years; the CPE period is always the two calendar years before the renewal year. 80 hours if you worked at a CPA firm, otherwise 60; half technical; 2 ethics.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE60every 2 years80 hours if you worked at least 3,000 hours at a licensed CPA firm during the period; otherwise 60 (201 KAR 1:100).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical standards30every 2 yearsAt least half the hours in technical standards courses.
Accounting & auditing each year8each yearApplies to: Attest or compilation/preparation work. If you perform attest or compilation/preparation work at a firm: 8 hours of auditing or accounting each calendar year.
Personal developmentUp to 12every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. Personal development is capped at 12 hours (8 if you need 80).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics2every 2 years2 hours of ethics each period. Courses don't have to be Kentucky-specific.

New licensees

First period: 2 hours for each full month your license was held (ethics waived)

Other limits

  • Teaching (with preparation): max 60% of the requirement; published articles: max 25%.
  • Nano-learning isn't addressed by the rule.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Kentucky State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
