Arizona CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 1, 2026

Board
Arizona State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Arizona registrations end on the last day of your birth month every two years (e.g. 03/31/2028).
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for three years.

How it works

Registration runs from the 1st of the month after your birth month to the last day of your birth month two years later.

Nano-learningUp to 4every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 4 hours of nano-learning count per registration period (R4-1-453).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics (AICPA Code + Arizona Board rules)4every 2 years4 hours, including at least 1 hour on the AICPA Code of Professional Conduct and 1 hour on Arizona Board statutes and rules. Not taught by your employer or a co-worker.

New licensees

Registration period shorter than two years: required hours are prorated by quarter (ethics isn't). The computer and nano-learning maximums apply per renewal period and aren't prorated.

Other limits

  • Authorship: max 20 hours; instruction plus authorship: max 40 hours.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Arizona State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
