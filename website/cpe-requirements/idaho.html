Idaho CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on September 28, 2026

Board
Idaho State Board of Accountancy (DOPL)
CPE required
80 hours over 2 calendar years
Renewal
01/31/2028 → 2026 and 2027. Idaho's CPE report is due each January 31.
Carryover
None

How it works

CPE is recorded by calendar year.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Two-year total80over 2 calendar yearsThe two calendar years before your date must reach 80 hours. Only 50 hours a year count toward it.
Minimum each year30each calendar yearAt least 30 hours every calendar year (at most 50 count).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4over 2 calendar years 

Other limits

  • Nano-learning counts 0.2 credit per 10 minutes.
  • Self-study without review questions or an end-of-course assessment doesn't qualify.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Idaho State Board of Accountancy (DOPL) whether that applies to you.
