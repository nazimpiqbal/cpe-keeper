Massachusetts CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 4, 2026

Board
Massachusetts Board of Public Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Massachusetts licenses expire June 30, every two years.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep certificates and course details; you sign a statement under penalty of perjury at renewal and the Board can ask for proof.

How it works

Licenses renew every two years on June 30. All 80 hours can be done in either year; no carryover.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics4every 2 years4 hours of professional ethics each renewal (252 CMR 2.14). Massachusetts doesn't require a state-specific course.

New licensees

First renewal prorated by the month your license was issued: July–Sept 80, Oct–Dec 70, Jan–Mar 60, Apr–June 50. The 4 ethics hours still apply.

Other limits

  • Instruction: no more than 50% of the requirement (40 of 80 hours) (NASBA summary of Massachusetts rules).
  • Published articles and books: no more than 20 hours (25% of 80).
  • University courses: 15 CPE per semester hour (credit set by the course).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Massachusetts Board of Public Accountancy whether that applies to you.
