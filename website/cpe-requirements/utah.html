Utah CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Utah Board of Accountancy (DOPL)
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Utah licenses expire December 31 of even-numbered years (e.g. 12/31/2026).
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE certificates; reporting goes through NASBA's CPE Audit Service.

How it works

Utah licenses and CPE run January 1 of an odd year to December 31 of the even year: 80 hours, including 4 ethics (1 on Utah law and rules). No yearly minimum.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each two-year cycle (R156-26a-303b). Up to 40 extra hours carry forward as general CPE.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4every 2 years4 hours of ethics each cycle, including the 1-hour Utah laws and rules course.
Utah laws & rules1every 2 years1 hour on Utah's CPA Licensing Act and Board rules.

New licensees

No CPE is required at your first renewal after initial licensure.

Other limits

  • The current rule has no caps on self-study, nano or instruction (some older summaries still list them).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Utah Board of Accountancy (DOPL) whether that applies to you.
