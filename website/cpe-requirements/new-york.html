New York CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on September 30, 2026

Board
New York State Education Department — State Board for Public Accountancy
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year (or 24 in one subject area)
Renewal
Your three-year registration end date. Your yearly CPE runs January–December regardless.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 5 years.

How it works

CPE is tracked January 1 – December 31 each year. No credits carry forward to the next year.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Annual CPE40each calendar yearRecognized subject areas (Education Law §7409(4)): accounting, attest, auditing, taxation, advisory services, specialized knowledge and applications related to specialized industries, and professional ethics. Ethics taken this year counts toward either option. No carryforward.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Attest competency40Applies to: Supervise attest/compilation or sign reports. If you supervise attest or compilation work or sign reports: 40 hours in accounting, auditing or attest in the three calendar years before the year you do the work, or in that year on its own (NYSED Q&A 5). The same hours also count toward your 24 or 40 for the year.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics4over 3 calendar yearsIf New York is your principal place of business, only an ethics course approved by NYSED is acceptable.

New licensees

New licensees start on the first January 1 that falls in their first registration period.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the New York State Education Department — State Board for Public Accountancy whether that applies to you.
