Colorado CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 4, 2026

Board
Colorado State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
December 31 of an odd-numbered year (your license renews the November 30 before it). Example: 12/31/2027.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for at least 5 years from the end of the year the CPE was completed.

How it works

Colorado's CPE reporting period runs January 1 of an even year to December 31 of the odd year. Licenses expire November 30 of the odd year.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years10 hours for every full quarter your certificate is active — 80 for a full reporting period.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Personal developmentUp to 16every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 20% of your CPE (16 of 80) can be personal development.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Colorado Rules & Regulations (CR&R)Up to 2every 2 yearsUp to 2 CR&R hours count toward the 4 ethics hours. CR&R beyond 2 hours counts as Specialized Knowledge, not ethics (Board CPE page).
Ethics4every 2 years4 hours of ethics; up to 2 of them can be Colorado Rules & Regulations (CR&R) — more CR&R doesn't count as ethics. Regulatory and behavioral ethics both count.

New licensees

First period: 10 hours for each full calendar quarter after your certificate was issued

Other limits

  • Teaching and publishing combined: max 50% (40 hours).
  • Internet courses: credit requires answering 75% of polling questions (shown on the certificate).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Colorado State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
