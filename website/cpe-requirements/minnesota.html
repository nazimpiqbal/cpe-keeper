Minnesota CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 4, 2026

Board
Minnesota Board of Accountancy
CPE required
120 hours over 3 calendar years
Renewal
Minnesota licenses renew every year on December 31. CPE years run July 1 – June 30 regardless.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for 5 years.

How it works

Minnesota CPE years run July 1 – June 30 and are reported by December 31. Each year needs 20 hours; the rolling three CPE years need 120, with 60 technical, 8 ethics and 24 group or blended.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Three-year total120over 3 calendar yearsThe three CPE years ending June 30 must reach 120 hours.
Minimum each year20each calendar yearAt least 20 hours every CPE year (July 1 – June 30), reported by December 31.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical60over 3 calendar yearsAt least 60 of the 120 from technical subjects (NASBA technical fields of study, including Regulatory Ethics).
Group or blended learning24over 3 calendar yearsAt least 24 of the 120 from group (live or online live) or blended programs.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics8over 3 calendar years8 hours of regulatory or behavioral ethics in the rolling three CPE years.

New licensees

No CPE is due for the CPE year you were licensed in; your first CPE year starts the next July 1.

Other limits

  • At least 72 of the 120 from approved CPE sponsors (NASBA Registry sponsors, peer-reviewed firms, universities, professional organizations).
  • Instruction: max 60 hours; published materials: max 60 hours.
  • The same self-study course can't be repeated for credit within one CPE year.
  • Carryback: hours after June 30 can count for the prior CPE year with late fees, but only once.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Minnesota Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
