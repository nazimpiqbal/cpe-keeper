Michigan CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 1, 2026

Board
Michigan Board of Accountancy (LARA)
CPE required
40 hours each year
Renewal
Michigan licenses renew July 31 every two years (e.g. 07/31/2027). CE years run July 1 – June 30.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CE records for 4 years after applying for renewal (R 338.5210).

How it works

Two CE years per license cycle, each July 1 – June 30. Licenses renew July 31.

Ethics each year2each year 
Michigan statutes & rules (ethics)1every 2 years1 of the 4 ethics hours in each two-year cycle must cover Michigan's accountancy statutes and rules (the MICPA course).

New licensees

Not required yet — no CE is due for the first 12 months after your original license.

Other limits

  • Teaching: max 20 hours per CE year (3 hours per 50 minutes).
  • No credit for repeating substantially equivalent content in the same period.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Michigan Board of Accountancy (LARA) whether that applies to you.
