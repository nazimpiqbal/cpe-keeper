North Carolina CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 4, 2026

Board
North Carolina State Board of CPA Examiners
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year
Renewal
North Carolina licenses renew every year by June 30. CPE is tracked by calendar year regardless.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for the current year and the four calendar years before it.

How it works

CPE is due by December 31 every year: 40 hours (2,000 minutes), including 1 hour of ethics. Up to 20 extra hours carry forward to the next year. You report at license renewal by June 30.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Annual CPE40each calendar year40 hours (2,000 minutes) by December 31 (21 NCAC 08G .0401). Up to 20 hours over last year's requirement carry forward.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics1each calendar yearAt least 50 minutes a year of regulatory or behavioral professional ethics from a NASBA-registered sponsor. Carried-forward hours can't meet it.

New licensees

The year your certificate was approved needs fewer hours: April–June 30, July–Sept 20, Oct–Dec 10 (21 NCAC 08G .0401). Courses earlier that year count.

Other limits

  • Instruction, college courses and CPE technical review combined: max 50% of the minutes.
  • Published materials: max 25% of the minutes.
  • Ethics minutes can't be carried forward to meet next year's ethics.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the North Carolina State Board of CPA Examiners whether that applies to you.
