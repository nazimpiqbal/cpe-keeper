Nevada CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Nevada State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year
Renewal
Nevada permits renew every year. CPE is tracked by calendar year.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 3 years after filing the renewal.

How it works

Since 2024, Nevada requires 40 hours every calendar year, including 2 of ethics. The permit renews every year.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Annual CPE40each calendar yearAt least 40 hours each calendar year (NAC 628.210).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Accounting & auditing8each calendar yearApplies to: Audits, reviews, full-disclosure compilations or attestation. In any year you work on an audit, review, full-disclosure compilation or attestation: 8 hours of accounting and auditing.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Professional ethics2each calendar year2 hours of professional ethics each year.

New licensees

Your first annual renewal of an initial permit is exempt.

Other limits

  • Instructor preparation: max twice the presentation time.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Nevada State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
