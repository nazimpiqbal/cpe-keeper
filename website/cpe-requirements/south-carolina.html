South Carolina CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
South Carolina Board of Accountancy
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year
Renewal
South Carolina licenses renew every year. CPE is tracked by calendar year.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for at least 5 years from the reporting due date.

How it works

South Carolina CPE runs by calendar year: 40 hours, 2 of them ethics. Up to 20 extra hours carry forward. The license renews every year.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Annual CPE40each calendar year40 hours each calendar year. Up to 20 hours over last year's 40 carry forward (not toward ethics).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Personal developmentUp to 8each calendar yearMaximum, not a target. Personal development is limited to 20% (8 hours) a year.
Nano-learningUp to 4each calendar yearMaximum, not a target. No more than 4 nano-learning hours a year.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics2each calendar yearAt least 2 hours of ethics every year. Since 2022 any behavioral or regulatory ethics course counts.

New licensees

No CPE is required in the year you're first licensed (and no carryover is earned that year).

Other limits

  • Authored works: max 10 hours; college instruction: max 10; committee/technical meetings: max 16.
  • At most 12 hours a day; blended learning isn't accepted.
  • Some kinds of hours (nano, authored works, higher education) may not carry forward.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the South Carolina Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
