Iowa CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Iowa Accountancy Examining Board
CPE required
120 hours over 3 calendar years
Renewal
Iowa licenses renew every July 1. CPE is tracked by calendar year.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 3 years after submitting the report that claims them.

How it works

Iowa licenses renew every July 1. CPE: 120 hours, 4 of them ethics, in the three years ending the December 31 before renewal (you may use June 30 instead). No yearly minimum.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Three-year total120over 3 calendar years120 hours in the three calendar years ending December 31 (481 IAC 1030.5). New licensees: 40 per full year licensed.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Financial statement presentation8over 3 calendar yearsApplies to: Supervise compilations or sign compilation reports. If you supervise compilations or sign compilation reports: 8 hours of financial statement presentation per three years.
Self-studyUp to 60over 3 calendar yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than half the hours (60) from self-study.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4over 3 calendar yearsAt least 4 hours of ethics and rules of professional conduct per three years.

Other limits

  • Non-technical skills, teaching and professional exams: each max 50%.
  • Meal meetings of 2 hours or less: max 25%.
  • You can use the three years ending June 30 instead of December 31.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Iowa Accountancy Examining Board whether that applies to you.
