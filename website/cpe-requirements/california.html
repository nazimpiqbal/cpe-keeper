California CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on September 30, 2026

Board
California Board of Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for 4 years.

How it works

80 hours in the two-year period immediately preceding license expiration; each year of the period measured back from the expiration date.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CE80every 2 years 
Total hours each year20each year 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical subject matter40every 2 years 
Technical each year12each year 
Non-technicalUp to 40every 2 yearsMaximum, not a target. Because 40 of the 80 hours must be technical, at most 40 non-technical hours can count toward the 80.
Accounting & auditing24every 2 yearsApplies to: Audit / attest (A&A). Financial statement preparation, auditing, reviews, compilations, attestation and assurance; governmental accounting and auditing courses count too. Fraud courses don't count toward these hours. Not needed if you also do government audits — the government hours cover A&A.
Governmental accounting & auditing24every 2 yearsApplies to: Government audits. Fraud courses don't count toward these hours. Meeting this and the fraud requirement also covers the A&A requirement.
Preparation engagement subject matter8every 2 yearsApplies to: Preparation engagements. 8 hours in preparation engagement subject matter. Covered by the A&A or government hours if you also do that work.
Fraud4every 2 yearsApplies to: Audit / attest (A&A); Government audits; Preparation engagements. 4 hours of fraud CE on top of the A&A, government or preparation-engagement hours (16 CCR 87(f)): the first 4 fraud hours count only here. Fraud hours beyond 4 can also count toward A&A or government when the course's field fits. One requirement, not one per activity.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4every 2 years4 hours of technical ethics (codes of conduct, ethical dilemmas, case studies). Only Regulatory Ethics counts; Behavioral Ethics is non-technical in California and doesn't count toward these 4.
Board-approved Regulatory Review course2once every 6 yearsRequired if more than six years have lapsed since the last one. Not interchangeable with ethics. Must carry a CBA approval number (RRL-/RRS-).

New licensees

First renewal only: 20 hours for each full six months from license issue to first expiration (none if under six full months). Half technical, including the 2-hour Regulatory Review. No yearly minimums. Ethics and fraud apply only once the full 80 hours is required.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the California Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
