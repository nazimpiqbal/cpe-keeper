Indiana CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Indiana Board of Accountancy
CPE required
120 hours every 3 years
Renewal
December 31 of the last year of your three-year period (2026, 2029, …). Your license renews the following June 30.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep certificates of completion for 36 months after the end of the renewal period.

How it works

Indiana CPE runs in three-calendar-year periods (now January 1, 2024 – December 31, 2026); licenses then renew the following June 30. 120 hours, at least 20 each year, 12 accounting & auditing, 4 ethics.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE120every 3 years120 hours in the three-year period (872 IAC 1-3).
Total hours each year20each year 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Accounting & auditing12every 3 yearsAt least 10% of your required hours (12 of a full 120) in accounting and auditing — required of every licensee.
Self-studyUp to 60every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than half your required hours (60 of a full 120) can be self-study.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4every 3 years4 hours of ethics each period (not prorated except for a certificate issued in the last quarter).

New licensees

Certificate issued during this period: hours depend on the quarter it was issued (872 IAC 1-3-16). Courses earlier in the period count

Other limits

  • Instruction: max 50% of the hours.
  • University credit: graduate-level courses only, grade C or better.
  • Ethics can also be met through verified unpaid work for a professional organization applying the code of conduct.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Indiana Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
