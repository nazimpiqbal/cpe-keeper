Missouri CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 4, 2026

Board
Missouri State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year
Renewal
The renewal date on your Missouri license. CPE is tracked by calendar year regardless.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for at least 5 calendar years after the year completed (20 CSR 2010-4.031).

How it works

Missouri CPE runs by calendar year: 40 hours each year, 2 of them ethics. Licensees in good standing can make up a shortage until March 1 of the next year.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Annual CPE40each calendar yearAt least 40 hours each calendar year (20 CSR 2010-4.010).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics2each calendar yearAt least 2 of the 40 hours each year in ethics. Regulatory and behavioral ethics both count.

New licensees

No CPE is due for the calendar year you were first licensed; the 40 hours start the next January 1.

Other limits

  • Authorship: max 2 hours a calendar year (20 CSR 2010-4.020).
  • March 1 grace: hours taken by March 1 to cure last year's shortage count for last year only — enter them in the year you report them.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Missouri State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
