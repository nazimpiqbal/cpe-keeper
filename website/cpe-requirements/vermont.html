Vermont CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Vermont Board of Public Accountancy (Office of Professional Regulation)
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Vermont licenses expire July 31 of odd-numbered years (e.g. 07/31/2027).
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for 5 years after each program (Rule 9.3(b)).

How it works

Vermont licenses renew July 31 of odd-numbered years: 80 hours over the two years, including 4 of ethics and 8 of accounting and auditing. Up to 10 extra hours carry forward.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each two-year period (Rule 9.1). Up to 10 hours over the 80 carry into the next period, toward the 80 only (not ethics or A&A).

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Accounting & auditing8every 2 yearsAll licensees: at least 8 hours of accounting and auditing each period (Rule 9.1(f)).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4every 2 yearsAt least 4 hours of ethics for accountants each period (Rule 9.1(e)).

Other limits

  • Instructor credit: max 50%; published articles and books: max 25%.
  • Only whole hours count (50 minutes = 1 hour).

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Vermont Board of Public Accountancy (Office of Professional Regulation) whether that applies to you.
