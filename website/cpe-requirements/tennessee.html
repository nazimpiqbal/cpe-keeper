Tennessee CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Tennessee State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
80 hours every 2 years
Renewal
Tennessee licenses renew every two years on December 31; your license tells you whether that's an odd or even year.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for five years after completion.

How it works

Tennessee CPE runs over two calendar years ending December 31 of your renewal year: 80 hours, at least 20 each year, 40 technical and a 2-hour Tennessee ethics course.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE80every 2 years80 hours each two-year period. Up to 24 hours over the 80 from your previous period carry forward — toward the 80 only, not the yearly minimums, technical hours or ethics.
Total hours each year20each year 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical40every 2 yearsAt least 40 of the 80 in technical subjects (NASBA technical fields of study).
Accounting & auditing20every 2 yearsApplies to: Attest services. If you provide attest services, at least 20 of the 40 technical hours in accounting and auditing.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Tennessee ethics2every 2 yearsA 2-hour Board-approved Tennessee state-specific ethics course every two-year period.

Other limits

  • Expert witnesses: at least 20 hours in the area the court deems you an expert.
  • New licensees licensed 1–2 years: carryforward is capped at 12 hours instead of 24.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Tennessee State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
