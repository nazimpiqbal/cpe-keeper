Washington CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on September 30, 2026

Board
Washington State Board of Accountancy
CPE required
120 hours every 3 years
Renewal
Washington licenses expire June 30 every three years (e.g. 06/30/2028). CPE is due by December 31 of the year before.
Carryover
None

How it works

Licenses expire June 30 every three years. CPE must be done by December 31 of the year before, over three calendar years.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Total CPE120every 3 yearsDue by December 31 of the year before your license expires. Renew between January 1 and April 30; the license lapses July 1 if not renewed.
Total hours each year20each year 

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical60every 3 yearsAt least 60 of the 120 must be technical, because no more than 60 non-technical hours count (WAC 4-30-134). Technical is everything except communication, personal development, client relations, practice development, motivational and speed-reading/memory courses — all ethics courses included.
Non-technicalUp to 60every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 60 non-technical hours count: communication, interpersonal and leadership skills, personal development, client and public relations, practice development, motivational courses, speed reading and memory building.
Nano-learningUp to 12every 3 yearsMaximum, not a target. No more than 12 hours of nano-learning count per reporting period (WAC 4-30-134).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Washington Board-approved ethics4every 3 yearsA 4-hour ethics and regulation course from a Washington Board-approved provider. Other ethics courses count toward the 120 but not this.

New licensees

Your first period runs from your license issue date to December 31 of the year before your license expires — the full 120 hours, at least 20 in each calendar year (including the first, partial one).

Other limits

  • Board service, first-time instruction/development and authorship: max 60 combined, and they don't count toward the 20-hour yearly minimum.
  • Non-interactive self-study earns half credit.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Washington State Board of Accountancy whether that applies to you.
