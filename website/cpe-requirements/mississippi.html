Mississippi CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Mississippi State Board of Public Accountancy
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year
Renewal
Mississippi licenses renew every year. CPE years run July 1 – June 30 regardless.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for at least 5 years after the end of the compliance year and triennium.

How it works

Mississippi compliance years run July 1 – June 30 (report by August 1): 40 hours a year, up to 20 carried from last year. Each triennium (now July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2028) also needs 3 hours of Board-approved ethics and 1 hour of Mississippi law and regulations.

Mississippi law & regulations1over 3 calendar years1 hour on Mississippi Public Accountancy Law and Regulations each triennium.

New licensees

No CPE is due in the compliance year you're licensed, and the ethics courses aren't due for the rest of that triennium.

Other limits

  • Self-study must be NASBA QAS-approved; independent study isn't accepted.
  • Teaching: preparation credit up to twice the presentation time.
  • Personal development over 20 doesn't carry forward.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Mississippi State Board of Public Accountancy whether that applies to you.
