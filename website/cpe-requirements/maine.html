Maine CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Maine Board of Accountancy (Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation)
CPE required
40 hours each calendar year
Renewal
Maine licenses renew every September 30.
Carryover
Allowed, with limits (see below)
Record keeping
Keep CPE documentation for the three license years before the current one (Ch. 5 §8(D)).

How it works

Maine licenses renew every September 30. Each license year (October 1 – September 30) needs 40 hours, and up to 20 extra carry into the next year. 4 hours of ethics every three years.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Annual CPE40each calendar year40 hours each license year (Ch. 5 §8(A)). Up to 20 hours over the 40 carry into the next license year only.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Limited subjectsUp to 20each calendar yearMaximum, not a target. No more than half (20 hours) from communication arts, mathematics and statistics, economics, business law, personnel and software applications (Ch. 5 §9(C)(4)(b)).

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4over 3 calendar yearsAt least 4 hours of ethics every three years: Maine accountancy laws and rules, or the AICPA or SEC conduct standards (Ch. 5 §8(B)).

New licensees

No CPE is due for the license year your license was first issued in; your first 40 hours are due at the end of the first full license year.

Other limits

  • Instructor credit: 3× class time for the first presentation only, max 50%.
  • Publications: max 50%.
  • CPA exam review courses and committee service don't count.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Maine Board of Accountancy (Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation) whether that applies to you.
