Montana CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Montana Board of Public Accountants (Department of Labor and Industry)
CPE required
120 hours over 3 calendar years
Renewal
Montana licenses renew every year by December 31.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for at least 5 years. Audits go through the NASBA CPE Audit Service.

How it works

Montana licenses renew every year by December 31. CPE: 120 hours over the three calendar years ending this December 31, at least 60 technical and 2 of ethics. No yearly minimum.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Three-year total120over 3 calendar years120 hours in the three calendar years ending December 31 (ARM 24.201.2106). Each hour counts in every three-year window it falls in.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical60over 3 calendar yearsAt least half (60 of 120) in technical subjects. Ethics counts as technical.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics2over 3 calendar years2 hours of ethics in the three years. No Montana-specific course is required.

New licensees

Licensed this year: nothing is due this year. Your first 120 hours are due December 31 of the third year after the year you were licensed (courses from the year licensed don't count).

Other limits

  • Self-study from sponsors not on the NASBA Registry earns half credit; their nano-learning earns none.
  • Instructor and technical reviewer credit: each max 60 hours; report reviewer: max 16 a year; publications: max 30 hours.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Montana Board of Public Accountants (Department of Labor and Industry) whether that applies to you.
