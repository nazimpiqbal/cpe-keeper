Oklahoma CPE requirements for CPAs

Checked against the board's rules on October 5, 2026

Board
Oklahoma Accountancy Board
CPE required
120 hours over 3 calendar years
Renewal
Oklahoma licenses renew every year on the last day of your birth month. CPE is tracked by calendar year.
Carryover
None
Record keeping
Keep CPE records for at least 5 years from the end of the year completed.

How it works

Oklahoma counts CPE by calendar year in a rolling three-year window: 120 hours and 4 ethics over any three years, at least 20 every year. Licenses renew yearly at the end of your birth month.

Requirements

Overall requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Three-year total120over 3 calendar yearsAny three consecutive calendar years must reach 120 hours (starting with the year you were certified).
Minimum each year20each calendar yearAt least 20 hours every calendar year — including the year you're first certified.

Subject requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Technical (permit holders)20each calendar yearApplies to: I hold a permit to practice. Permit holders: at least 20 technical hours each year (not in the year the permit is first issued).
Nano-learningUp to 4each calendar yearMaximum, not a target. Up to 20 nano courses (4 hours) count each year.

Special requirements

RequirementHoursDetails
Ethics4over 3 calendar yearsAt least 4 hours of ethics in each rolling three-year period.

Other limits

  • CPAs in industry: 72 of the 120 in subjects related to public accounting, plus 8 a year in accounting, tax or assurance.
  • Compilation permit holders: 4 hours of compilation CPE each year.
  • Published materials: max 10 hours a year; self-study must be interactive.

Licensed in more than one state?

Many boards let CPAs whose main office is in another state meet CPE through their home state's rules instead, sometimes with extras such as a state ethics course. Check with the Oklahoma Accountancy Board whether that applies to you.
